Privacy Policy — Mille Playlist Uploader

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Mille Playlist Uploader ("the tool") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Mille Playlist YouTube channel to manage that channel. The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • The authorized channel's basic information (channel ID, title).
  • The channel's own videos: upload, title, description, tags, thumbnail, localizations, privacy status and publish schedule.
  • Public statistics and analytics of the channel's own videos (views, watch time, click-through rate).

The tool does not access other users' accounts, private data, comments of other channels for collection, or any data unrelated to the owner's channel.

How the data is used

Data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos and to review how those videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens and video metadata are stored only on the owner's own computer. Statistics may be kept as local files for up to 30 days and are then refreshed or deleted. No data is stored on a public server.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the stored tokens and locally saved data are deleted.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Mille Playlist YouTube channel.
