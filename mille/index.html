Mille Playlist Uploader

A personal upload tool for the Mille Playlist YouTube channel.

This tool is used only by the owner of the Mille Playlist YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own playlist videos, sets their title, description, thumbnail, tags, translations and publish schedule, and reads basic statistics for the channel's own videos.

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.
